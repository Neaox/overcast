[appregistry] `ListApplications` and `DeleteApplication` no longer leak `tags`/`applicationTag`, `CreateAttributeGroup`/`UpdateAttributeGroup` no longer leak `attributes`, `ListAttributeGroups`/`DeleteAttributeGroup` no longer leak `tags`/`attributes`, and associated-resource entries no longer carry a `creationTime` — the AppRegistry model binds these operations to `ApplicationSummary`, `AttributeGroup`, `AttributeGroupSummary` and `ResourceInfo` respectively, narrower shapes than the ones the emulator was reusing verbatim from Create/Get/Update
