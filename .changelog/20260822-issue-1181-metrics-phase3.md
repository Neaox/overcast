+ [web/lambda] Lambda function pages now have a Monitor tab showing the AWS/Lambda metrics the emulator already records automatically (phase 1 of the service-metrics substrate, #1230) as charts — Invocations, Errors, Duration (average/maximum), Throttles, and Concurrent Executions — over 1h/6h/24h/7d/30d ranges, reading through a new narrow `GET /_overcast/lambda/functions/{name}/metrics` allowlist endpoint (never CloudWatch protocol) and the new BFF proxy `GET /api/lambda/functions/{name}/metrics`.
+ [web/sqs] SQS queue pages now have a Monitor section showing the AWS/SQS metrics the emulator already records automatically (phase 2 of the service-metrics substrate, #1268) as charts — messages sent/received/deleted/empty-receives and the queue-depth and oldest-message-age gauges — over the same five ranges, through `GET /_overcast/sqs/queues/{name}/metrics` and the BFF proxy `GET /api/sqs/queues/{name}/metrics`.
+ [metrics] The service-metrics substrate now stores a 300-second (7-day) and 3600-second (30-day) resolution rollup alongside the existing 1-minute tier, whose own retention grows from 1 hour to 24 hours — the full ladder the design always specified, so a 6h/24h/7d/30d Monitor chart has real data instead of only the last hour.
