* [cloudformation] a rollback that cannot delete what it built now reports it. Around fifty resource handlers discarded the result of their delete and reported success unconditionally, so a rollback recorded `DELETE_COMPLETE` and the stack reached `ROLLBACK_COMPLETE` with the resource still standing. They now report the failure, and the stack reaches `ROLLBACK_FAILED` with the resource left in its list — which is the signal an operator needs, and what real CloudFormation does. A resource that is merely already gone stays a successful teardown, so nothing can wedge over a resource that no longer exists.
