~ [web] CloudFront's six list pages — Distributions, Continuous Deployment Policies,
  Field-Level Encryption Configs and Profiles, Key Groups, Realtime Log Configs — sort by
  clicking a column header, and the sorted view deep-links as `?sort=id` / `?sort=-id`.
  Distributions also gains a Columns menu for hiding the secondary columns, and the
  Origins, Origin Groups and Invalidations tables on a distribution's detail page sort
  the same way.
